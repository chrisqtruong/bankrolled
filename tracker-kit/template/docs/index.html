Confirmed, reported, disputed

Confirmed: backed by a primary record or by reputable reporting that cites one. Reported: credible but not yet verifiable. Disputed: reported and publicly denied by the subject. Only confirmed items enter the ledger. Lawsuits and charges are allegations. When sources disagree on a number, the number is left out.

Known blind spots

  • Some websites block automated readers; we retry through a second tool and note any page we could not open in the daily report.
  • Paywalled outlets often show only a headline. We then look for a primary record or a second outlet, or label the item reported.
  • Very large public records can be too big to read in full; we use official data feeds where they exist.
  • We do not use social media posts as sources.
  • Hourly checks and search lag mean some stories arrive late; late finds keep their true date and are marked in the report. Small and local outlets are the likeliest to be missed.

If a story is missing here but reported elsewhere, it should surface from a second source or a primary record. Tips with a source are welcome.

How it updates

A scheduled research run checks primary sources and the news every hour, verifies each item at its source, and commits the result to the project repository. Nothing is deleted; corrections are made in place and noted.
